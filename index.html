FloodSentinnel

Team VitaForge · Prototype demo
🏗️ ONE CORE ENGINE · TWO SUPPORTING WINDOWS

A village-level flood alarm — extended to two urban problems it wasn't built for.

FloodSentinnel monitors a river or drain, decides how dangerous the situation is, and pushes that decision out as a siren, an SMS, and a dashboard alert. The same decision engine is reused, unmodified in concept, for two other problems: drain blockages (DrainWatch) and water-network leaks (JalSetu). This is a working prototype — every control below actually computes something. Nothing here is a static screenshot.

🚨FloodSentinel — Core

River/drain water level + rainfall → GREEN→YELLOW→ORANGE→RED risk engine → siren + SMS + dashboard, plus resilient-housing guidance.

🌀DrainWatch

Drain/inlet blockage sensing → maintenance ticket routed to the ULB or panchayat, with a resolve flow. Monitoring and action.

🛰️JalSetu

Water-network pressure/flow anomaly → localized to a zone (not a pipe) → the same kind of action ticket, to the water utility.

🎯Three things worth actually doing, not just reading

1. Cascade a flood

On the Core screen, drag the river-level or rainfall slider (or hit "Run scenario") and watch the risk state climb through all four stages in real time — with the exact siren/SMS/dashboard copy that fires at each one.

2. Close a drain ticket

On DrainWatch, push a drain's blockage past threshold, watch a ticket appear in the action queue, then mark it resolved.

3. Localize a leak

On JalSetu, trigger a pressure anomaly and see it land in the same ticket queue used by DrainWatch — proof it's one engine, reused.

  • ~75 lakh hectares affected per year
  • Annual economic loss ~₹15,000–20,000+ crore (SBI, 2023)
  • World Bank: ~US$9.8B total extreme-weather loss; floods ≈ 50% of that

  • India's most flood-prone state
  • 76% of North Bihar's population under recurring flood threat
  • 73% of Bihar's geographic area affected
  • Bihar = 22.1% of India's flood-affected population
  • 9,500+ lives lost in Bihar since 1979

Five-layer solution stack

1
Detection — ESP32, water-level / rainfall / flow-rise sensing.
2
Alert — rule-based deterministic baseline + XGBoost/LSTM forecasting layer (simulated in this prototype), siren + SMS.
3
Infrastructure — flood-resilient housing: raised plinth, flood vents, RCC frames.
4
Shelter — elevated multipurpose evacuation centers, supply and livestock storage.
5
Diversion (additional innovation, genuinely more novel than the alarm/housing layers) — floodwater storage channels, reservoir redirection, drought-region reuse.

Before you explore FloodSentinnel

This is a working prototype, opened without anyone narrating it. Three quick things before you start:

1. One engine, three windows

FloodSentinel Core (rural + urban flood risk) is the engine. DrainWatch and JalSetu reuse the exact same risk-and-action logic for drains and water networks.

2. Everything reacts

Every slider, button, and ticket is live. Nudge a sensor value and watch the state actually cascade — nothing here is a screenshot standing in for behavior.

3. We say what's simulated

Sensor feeds, siren/SMS delivery, and the ML forecast are simulated and clearly badged. The risk logic, ticket queue, and every number on the Info page are real.

This project's own research scores its novelty at 5.5/10 — full honest positioning is one tap away on the Info & Sources page, any time.

How this works

FloodSentinnel is one risk-decision engine, used three times:

  • FloodSentinel Core — river/drain level + rainfall → GREEN/YELLOW/ORANGE/RED → siren + SMS + dashboard.
  • DrainWatch — same engine, fed by drain-blockage % instead → opens a maintenance ticket.
  • JalSetu — same engine again, fed by pressure/flow anomaly → localizes to a zone, opens a ticket.
